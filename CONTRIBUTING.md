# Contributing

## Our commitment

We are commited to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectfull, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identification or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimintation, or intimidation.
- Insults, personal attacks, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that prevents others from participating.
- Any other conduct that would bee inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **[Lukeskywalker4621@gmail.com]**. Include as much context as you can, such as links, screenshots, or dates. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcments

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, or any other contributions that violate this code. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Maintainers will explain enforcments decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project or its community.
